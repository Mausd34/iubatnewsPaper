Who we are

IUBAT Campus News is the independent student newspaper of the International University of Business Agriculture and Technology (IUBAT). We are a student-led editorial project covering campus news, events, sports, academics, clubs, student achievements and official notices — the stories that matter to our community.

Our sections

  • Campus News — what's happening across the university
  • Events — seminars, fairs, cultural programs and deadlines
  • Sports — fixtures, results and athletes of IUBAT
  • Academic — research, exams, results and classroom stories
  • Clubs — student club activities and volunteering
  • Achievements — awards, competitions and alumni success
  • Notice Board — official announcements

How contributions work

Any registered student can become a Student Contributor. Submissions enter an editorial approval queue. Faculty authors and editors can publish directly. Every published article carries the author's byline and builds a public writing portfolio on their profile.

Editorial guidelines

  1. Be accurate — verify names, dates and quotes before submitting.
  2. Be fair — give people a chance to respond to criticism.
  3. Be original — no plagiarism, and credit all images and sources.
  4. Be respectful — no harassment, hate speech or personal attacks.
  5. Declare conflicts of interest.

Contact the editorial team

Got a news tip, a correction or a press release? Visit our contact page or email news@iubatnews.edu.
